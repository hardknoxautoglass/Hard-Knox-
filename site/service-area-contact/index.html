Service Area, Hours & Contact

Hard Knox Auto Glass is a mobile-only service for Hamilton County, Tennessee. There is no shop to visit. Check that we cover your area below, then call, text or email for a free, no-obligation quote.

Tapping Call or Text opens your phone's dialer or messaging app with the number filled in; nothing is sent until you send it. On a computer without a phone app, select the number above and copy it.

Email

Email is the slower route; calls and texts get the quickest reply. If you prefer email, write to HardKnoxAutoGlass@gmail.com (HardKnoxAutoGlass@gmail.com). Please keep insurance documents, card details and other sensitive information out of email, too.

Hours

Monday–Saturday, 7:30 AM–6:00 PM Eastern Time. Closed Sunday. These are the hours for calls, texts and email replies; a particular appointment is subject to availability, and Brandon will offer times when he confirms your quote.

What to send for a quote

  • Year, make and model of the vehicle
  • Which glass is damaged: windshield, a door window, a quarter window or the back glass
  • What happened and what you see (a photo of a chip or crack helps, though it is not a diagnosis)
  • Whether the windshield has a camera or sensor housing behind the mirror
  • The general area where the vehicle will be, such as the town or neighborhood, and whether it is a driveway, a workplace lot or another spot
  • Days and times that suit you

Please do not send a full VIN, a license plate, your exact home address, insurance documents or any payment details in a first text. Brandon confirms the exact address by phone when the work is scheduled.

Just outside Hamilton County?

If you are in a community directly adjacent to Hamilton County, call or text to confirm availability for your location. Any mobile/travel charge is quoted before service. Within Hamilton County, mobile service is part of the quoted price. A free quote means the quote costs nothing; the final price is confirmed once the vehicle, the work, the location and any travel charge are known, and before any work is scheduled.

Where the work happens

Brandon comes to your home, your workplace or another approved location: a reasonably level spot with room to open the doors and hood, sheltered from heavy rain and strong wind, where the vehicle can stay parked while the work is done. He confirms the exact address by phone when the job is scheduled, so there is no need to put it in a first text. Weather or unsuitable site conditions can require rescheduling; there is no cancellation fee. More on suitable locations. ADAS calibration has its own requirements, explained on the ADAS calibration page.

Need another way to reach us?

If a phone call is difficult for you, text or email works just as well for quotes, scheduling and warranty questions. See the accessibility page for more about the help available and how to report a problem with this site.

What happens with your details

Calls, texts and emails go to Brandon and are used to answer you and arrange the work. The privacy notice explains what is kept, who provides the phone and email services, and how to ask a question about your information.
