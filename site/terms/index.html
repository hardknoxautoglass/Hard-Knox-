Website Terms of Use

Effective November 4, 2026.

These terms cover the use of HardKnoxAutoGlass.com and explain how a quote inquiry becomes authorized work. They are deliberately short. They do not override the limited guarantee, the cancellation and refund policy, or any right you have under Tennessee or federal law.

1. Who runs this site

HardKnoxAutoGlass.com is the website of Hard Knox Auto Glass, a veteran-owned mobile auto glass service in Hamilton County, Tennessee. Contact details are on the contact page.

2. What the information on this site is for

The pages describe the services offered, how they work, where they are offered, and the guarantee and policies that apply. They are general information to help you decide what to ask for. They are not a diagnosis of your vehicle, and nothing on the site tells you whether a particular chip can be repaired, whether your vehicle needs calibration, or whether a vehicle is safe to drive; those decisions are made for your specific vehicle, in person, by the technician. We try to keep the site accurate and current; if you find something that looks wrong, please tell us.

3. Quote inquiries

Calling, texting or emailing for a quote is free and places you under no obligation. A quote inquiry is a request for information, not an order. Hard Knox Auto Glass confirms the vehicle, the scope of work, the service location, the price, any mobile/travel charge outside Hamilton County, and availability with you before anything is scheduled. A quote is based on what you describe; if the vehicle or the damage turns out to be different when inspected in person, you will be told before any work starts and you can decide how to proceed.

4. How work is authorized

Work begins only when you have agreed to the confirmed scope and price for your vehicle. Your agreement is given to the owner directly, by phone, text or in person, and is recorded on the work order or invoice for the job. A quote inquiry is never authorization for additional work. If something extra is found during the job, for example a damaged window regulator behind a broken door glass, or rust under an old windshield, you are told first, any additional work is quoted separately, and it is done only if you authorize it.

5. Payment

Payment is by card or cash and is due upon completion of the work. There is no cancellation fee. Details, including what happens when weather or site conditions force a reschedule, are in the cancellation, rescheduling and refund policy.

6. Guarantee

Work is covered by the Hard Knox Auto Glass Limited Warranty / Service Guarantee, which is published in full and available on paper before you authorize work. Nothing in these website terms reduces that guarantee.

7. Insurance

Hard Knox Auto Glass does not file insurance claims, does not advise on what a policy covers or what a deductible is, and makes no representation about what an insurer will approve, pay or reimburse. You are welcome to use a Hard Knox quote or invoice with your insurer; any claim or reimbursement is between you and your insurer, and the price for the work is due to Hard Knox at completion.

8. Using the site

  • You may read, print and link to pages on this site for your own use, including printing the guarantee for your records.
  • The text, illustrations, icons and layout of this site belong to Hard Knox Auto Glass unless otherwise noted (the heading typeface is used under the SIL Open Font License). Please do not copy them for another business.
  • Do not attempt to interfere with the site or its hosting.
  • Links to outside sites are provided for convenience; Hard Knox Auto Glass does not control those sites.

9. Privacy and accessibility

How information is handled is described in the privacy notice. Help using the site and how to report a barrier are on the accessibility page.

10. Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time; the effective date at the top will change when they are. The version on this page is the current one.
