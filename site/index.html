Questions

Straight answers before you call

What services does Hard Knox Auto Glass offer?

Six things: windshield replacement, windshield rock chip repair, door glass replacement, quarter glass replacement, back glass replacement, and ADAS calibration for vehicles that need it after a windshield is replaced. All glass work is done at your location. Compare the services.

Is this a shop I can drive to? How do I get a quote?

No. Hard Knox Auto Glass is mobile only. There is no shop, storefront, waiting room or walk-in address; Brandon comes to your home, workplace or another suitable location in Hamilton County, Tennessee.

To get a free, no-obligation quote, call or text (865) 299-3533 with your vehicle's year, make and model, which glass is damaged and the general area where the vehicle will be. For your security, please do not text payment-card, bank-account, insurance-policy, claim, or other sensitive information.

Where can the work be done?

Most glass work needs a reasonably level, safe spot with room to open the doors and the hood, out of heavy rain or strong wind: a driveway, a workplace parking space you are allowed to use, or a similar location. Brandon confirms that the spot will work when he quotes the job. Weather or unsuitable site conditions can require rescheduling. ADAS calibration has its own site and vehicle requirements, explained on the ADAS calibration page.

What should I send for a quote?

Year, make and model; which glass is damaged; what you see (a photo of a chip or crack helps but is not a diagnosis); whether there is a camera or sensor housing behind the mirror; the general area where the vehicle will be; and days that suit you. Please leave out your full VIN, plate, exact home address, insurance documents and payment details. What to have ready.

How do I pay, and is there a cancellation fee?

Card or cash, due when the work is complete. There is no cancellation fee. Quotes are free and carry no obligation. Cancellation, rescheduling and refund policy.

What are your hours?

Monday–Saturday, 7:30 AM–6:00 PM Eastern Time. Those are the hours for calls and texts; a particular appointment is subject to availability.

I'm just outside Hamilton County. Is travel included?

Call or text first to confirm that Brandon can come to you. Within Hamilton County, mobile service is part of the quoted price. Outside the county, any mobile/travel charge is quoted before service. A free quote means the quote itself costs nothing; it does not set the final cost until the vehicle, the work, the location and any travel charge have been confirmed. Service area details.

Is a new rock strike covered by the guarantee for as long as I own the car?

No. The limited guarantee covers defects in Hard Knox's installation workmanship and in glass Hard Knox supplies and installs, for as long as the original customer owns the vehicle. New damage from road debris, rocks, accidents, vandalism, misuse or other outside events is not a defect and is not covered. What the guarantee does not cover.

My repaired chip spread. Do I get a cash refund?

No cash refund; you get a credit. If a chip Hard Knox repaired spreads, fails an applicable state inspection, or you are simply not satisfied with the repair, the amount you paid for that repair is applied toward a full windshield replacement by Hard Knox. The benefit belongs to the original customer. A cash refund is a different remedy that applies only when a covered replacement problem cannot be corrected. Chip repair credit terms.

Can you come to my driveway for ADAS calibration?

Sometimes. Hard Knox Auto Glass offers ADAS calibration, but whether it can be done in a driveway depends on your vehicle, the procedure its manufacturer requires (static, dynamic or both) and the site. Some procedures need a level, well-lit space with room for targets; some are completed by driving the vehicle on suitable roads; some need a different location. Call or text with your year, make, model and general location, and Brandon will confirm the arrangements for your vehicle before anything is scheduled. ADAS calibration explained.

Can I call after 30 days about a defect?

Yes. Hard Knox asks you to call as soon as you notice a problem, preferably within 30 days of noticing it, with your invoice or other proof of service handy, because early reporting makes the cause easier to find and fix. Reporting later does not by itself cancel coverage. Covered defects are covered for as long as the original customer owns the vehicle. If you cannot make a voice call, text or email instead. How to report a problem.

Do you work with my insurance company?

You are welcome to use a Hard Knox quote or invoice with your insurer, but Hard Knox does not file claims, cannot tell you what your policy covers or what your deductible is, and does not make promises about what an insurer will pay or reimburse. Our price is due at completion; any claim or reimbursement is between you and your insurer.
