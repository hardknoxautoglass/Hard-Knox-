No verifiable, currently-operating business in the target categories could be confirmed as physically headquartered in Signal Mountain, Lookout Mountain TN, Walden, or Sale Creek — these are small, largely residential communities; several businesses above serve them (e.g., multiple tree-service companies market "Signal Mountain service") but are legitimately based in Chattanooga or Hixson, not in those towns themselves, so they aren't counted there.

## 4. Research Notes & Limitations

**Method:** Research was conducted by six parallel research passes, each covering a distinct industry cluster, using web search against official company websites, Google/Yelp business listings, BBB profiles, Chamber of Commerce and trade-association directories, FMCSA/carrier records, and news coverage. Results were then consolidated, deduplicated, and filtered against the verification checklist in this project's brief.

**Filtering applied during compilation:**
- Every row required a real Hamilton County street address (not a PO box, not an out-of-county HQ) and a verified phone number. Roughly 45 additional candidates that the research passes surfaced were **dropped** from this list because a phone number or a resolvable street address could not be confirmed within the available search budget (this included several small janitorial/cleaning companies, a few small funeral homes, some freight brokerages, and a couple of security/fire-protection branch offices). They were excluded rather than included with guessed contact information.
- A small number of candidates were dropped for other verification failures: conflicting/unverifiable data (e.g., a beverage distributor with two contradictory phone numbers in different sources), an address that only specified a city with no confirmable street address, and one collision-repair chain that itself performs glass replacement in-house (a competitor, not a prospect).
- "Estimated Fleet Size" is left as **"Not publicly verified"** for the large majority of businesses because no business publicly discloses an exact vehicle count. The 12 exceptions show a figure that was explicitly published by the company or a reputable secondary source (e.g., City Auto's ~889-vehicle inventory, Covenant Logistics' ~2,300 tractors per its public-company profile, Brown Bros.' 100+ employees). No fleet size was estimated or guessed.
- Emails are shown only where an address was actually published by the business (12 of 140). All others are marked "Not publicly available" rather than a guessed format.
- Decision-maker names are shown only where publicly attributed to a specific person by the business's own site, BBB, or another public record (23 of 140, mostly owners of smaller independent shops). Larger dealerships, corporate branches, and government departments generally do not publish an individual contact, and none was guessed.

**Environment constraint:** Direct page-fetching (visiting a company's website to read it live) was blocked by network restrictions in this research environment for most of the process; verification relied on indexed search-engine snippets of those same official sites plus independent directories (BBB, Yelp, Chamber, FMCSA/SAFER, corporate locators, news). This is a real but disclosed limitation — treat phone numbers and addresses here as search-engine-verified and cross-referenced, and do a final live check (call the number, confirm the site) immediately before outreach, as recommended for any prospecting list.

**What this means for next steps:** This is a strong, quality-controlled starting list, not an exhaustive one. Categories most likely to have additional un-surfaced prospects: small independent trades contractors (roofing/plumbing/electrical), commercial cleaning/janitorial firms, funeral homes, and freight brokerages/small trucking outfits — all industries where many legitimate small operators exist without a strong web/SEO footprint. A follow-up research pass focused specifically on those categories, with live site verification, would likely add meaningful volume without compromising accuracy.
